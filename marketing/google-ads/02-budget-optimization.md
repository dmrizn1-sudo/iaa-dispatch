# Budget Optimization Plan — Israel Air Ambulance

**North star:** Minimize Cost Per Qualified Lead (CPQL) for private international medical flights **TO Israel** and **FROM Israel**.

---

## 1. Budget philosophy

| Principle | Rule |
|-----------|------|
| Intent first | Fund high-intent Search (TO / FROM / Repatriation / ICU / Emergency) before broad themes |
| Geo quality > volume | Priority-1 countries get majority of spend until CPQL proves Wave 2 |
| Protect brand | Always fund Brand Search (cheap, high conversion) |
| Kill waste fast | Pause queries/geos that spend without qualified calls within 14 days |
| Cap experimentation | Max 10% of monthly B for new themes / Wave-3 geos |

---

## 2. Recommended monthly allocation (share of B)

| Bucket | Share | Campaigns |
|--------|-------|-----------|
| Core demand | 35% | TO Israel, Medical Repatriation, Emergency Medical Flight, Fly Patient Home, Emergency Patient Return |
| High AOV critical care | 18% | ICU Air Ambulance, Private Air Ambulance |
| FROM Israel | 14% | Air Ambulance FROM Israel |
| Escort ladder | 14% | Medical Escort, Commercial Flight Medical Escort, Stretcher |
| Support themes | 6% | International Patient Transfer, Bed-to-Bed |
| Brand | 5% | Brand Search |
| Testing reserve | 8% | City ad groups, Wave-2 geos, RSA tests |

### Example absolute budgets

| Monthly B | Core | ICU/Private | FROM | Escort | Support | Brand | Test |
|-----------|------|-------------|------|--------|---------|-------|------|
| $5,000 | $1,750 | $900 | $700 | $700 | $300 | $250 | $400 |
| $10,000 | $3,500 | $1,800 | $1,400 | $1,400 | $600 | $500 | $800 |
| $20,000 | $7,000 | $3,600 | $2,800 | $2,800 | $1,200 | $1,000 | $1,600 |

Adjust currency to account billing; keep **shares** stable.

---

## 3. Daily pacing rules

1. No non-brand campaign > **25%** of daily budget until it is top-3 CPQL for 14 days.
2. If a campaign spends 80% of daily budget by 14:00 local with <1 qualified lead → reduce bids or pause weak ad groups same day.
3. Brand: never let lost IS (budget) exceed 5%.
4. Soft-launch: Priority-1 geos only for first 14 days.

---

## 4. Reallocation triggers (weekly)

| Signal | Action |
|--------|--------|
| Campaign CPQL ≤ 80% of account median | +15–25% budget |
| Campaign CPQL ≥ 150% of median + ≥$200 spend | −20% or pause weak ad groups |
| Country CPQL winner (min 3 qualified leads) | + bid modifier +10–20% |
| Country $150+ spend, 0 qualified | Exclude or −50% bids |
| Mobile call CVR > desktop by 30%+ | +10–20% mobile bids |
| Device with high spend, low calls | −20% device bid |

---

## 5. 90-day budget phases

### Phase A (Days 1–30) — Learn
- 100% Search; Exact + Phrase
- Maximize Conversions (or Manual CPC if <15 conv/month)
- Daily search-term mining
- Target: establish baseline CPQL by campaign

### Phase B (Days 31–60) — Scale winners
- Shift 10–15% from underperformers to TO / ICU / Repatriation winners
- Enable top 10 city ad groups with impressions
- Introduce Wave-2 geos at ≤15% of B

### Phase C (Days 61–90) — Efficient growth
- tCPA if ≥30 conversions / 30 days account-wide
- Consider limited Performance Max **only** with asset groups pointing to international LPs + strong negatives (optional)
- Hold Display/YouTube until Search CPQL is stable

---

## 6. What not to fund

- Domestic ground ambulance keywords
- Medical tourism landing pages from Ads
- Broad Match without conversion volume
- Low-income / high-fraud geos in `geo.json` exclude list
- Awareness Display for “brand building” without lead tracking
