# City SEO Plan — Israel Air Ambulance

**Goal:** Capture high-intent city + Israel medical flight queries from major international gateways.

---

## 1. URL model

`/routes/{country}/{city}`  
Examples: `/routes/usa/new-york`, `/routes/greece/athens`, `/routes/uae/dubai`

Israel cities (Tel Aviv, Jerusalem, Haifa) appear as **destination context** on TO Israel pages and in copy — not as “local EMS” city pages.

---

## 2. City page template (600–1,000 words)

1. **H1:** `Air Ambulance {City} to Israel` (or bidirectional if FROM demand)  
2. Supporting line: private medical flights · ICU / escort options · 24/7  
3. Why families search this route (tourism, business, relatives abroad)  
4. Service options: private air ambulance · commercial medical escort · stretcher  
5. Bedside-to-bedside logistics note  
6. Link to parent country page + core service LPs  
7. FAQ (2–3)  
8. CTA Call / WhatsApp

## 4. On-page SEO checklist

- [ ] Unique title/meta (no country-page clone)  
- [ ] H1 includes city + Israel  
- [ ] 1 real visual (aircraft/crew) with descriptive ALT  
- [ ] Internal links to country + TO/FROM/ICU/Escort  
- [ ] LocalAirport schema optional; MedicalBusiness on sitewide  
- [ ] No “ambulance near me” / domestic EMS language  

---

## 5. Keyword mapping rule

| Query pattern | Landing page |
|---------------|--------------|
| `{city} to israel` air ambulance | City page |
| `{country} to israel` (no city) | Country page |
| `air ambulance to israel` (generic) | `/air-ambulance-to-israel` |
| `icu air ambulance` | `/icu-air-ambulance` |

Avoid cannibalization: one primary URL per query cluster.

Pair each new city page with 1 social post + optional GBP post referencing the route (no auto-publish).
