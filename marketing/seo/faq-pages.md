## Global FAQs (all international LPs)

### Do you operate air ambulance TO Israel and FROM Israel?
Yes. Israel Air Ambulance focuses on private international medical flights **to Israel** and **from Israel**, including ICU air ambulance, stretcher flights, and commercial medical escort when clinically appropriate.

### Do you provide domestic ground ambulance in Israel via these pages?
These international pages are for **cross-border medical flights**. Domestic private ambulance is a separate service line and is not what Google Ads international campaigns promote.

### Who are your typical clients?
Private families and patients — including travelers, Israelis abroad, foreign citizens needing transfer to Israel, and patients requiring ICU or escorted transport after hospitalization abroad.

### What is bedside-to-bedside transport?
We coordinate the continuum from the origin bedside (hospital or home) through ground transfer, the medical flight, and handoff to the receiving hospital bed.

### Air ambulance vs medical escort — what’s the difference?
**Air ambulance:** dedicated medical aircraft configured for critical care.  
**Medical escort:** a doctor or paramedic accompanies the patient on a commercial flight when clinically suitable — often more cost-effective.

### How fast can you respond?
Coordination is available **24/7**. Timing depends on clinical readiness, aircraft/escort availability, permits, and receiving hospital confirmation. Urgent cases are prioritized.

### How much does a private air ambulance cost?
Cost varies by distance, aircraft type, medical team, ground logistics, and urgency. We provide a clear assessment after understanding the patient’s condition and route — we do not publish misleading fixed online prices.

### Can you fly ICU patients?
Yes. ICU-capable configurations may include ventilation, monitoring, infusion pumps, airway equipment, and medications matched to the patient, staffed by appropriate flight medical clinicians.

### Do you work with insurance companies or hospitals as the primary customer?
Our marketing focus is **private families**. We can coordinate with hospitals or insurers when they support a private family’s transfer, but campaigns are not optimized for tenders or B2B bulk contracts.

### How do I start?
Call or WhatsApp **+972-79-670-9999** (24/7) or submit the short form on this page. Have patient location, destination, and a brief clinical summary ready.
