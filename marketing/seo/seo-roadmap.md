## 3. 90-day SEO plan

### Days 1–30 — Foundation
- Publish/repair core service pages (TO, FROM, Repatriation, ICU, Escort, Contact).
- Fix technical: indexation, sitemap, canonicals, hreflang (EN + future FR/DE), Core Web Vitals.
- Rewrite homepage hierarchy so **international air ambulance** is the hero for EN international traffic (domestic products secondary/deeper).
- NAP consistency: Israel Air Ambulance / Israel Air & Ambulance + phone + WhatsApp.
- Google Business Profile: categories aligned to air/medical transport; avoid misleading local-only EMS.

### Days 31–60 — Authority content
Publish 6–8 guides (1,200–1,800 words), each with CTA:

1. Air Ambulance vs Medical Escort — Which Does My Family Need?  
2. How Medical Repatriation to Israel Works  
3. ICU Air Ambulance: What Families Should Expect  
4. Documents & Fit-to-Fly for International Medical Flights  
5. Medical Flight from Greece / Cyprus to Israel (holiday emergencies)  
6. Cost Factors in Private Air Ambulance (no fake prices)  
7. Bedside-to-Bedside Transport Explained  
8. USA ↔ Israel Patient Transfer Guide  

Internal link each guide → service LP → Contact.

### Days 61–90 — Geo expansion
- Launch route pages for USA, UK, France, Germany, Greece, Cyprus, UAE, Thailand.
- Add city sections for New York, Miami, London, Paris, Athens, Larnaca, Dubai, Bangkok.
- Digital PR: aviation/medical travel mentions; family stories with consent.
- Review Search Console: query → page mapping; fix cannibalization.

---

## 4. On-page template

- Title ≤ 60 chars · Meta ≤ 155  
- H1 unique · H2s for sub-intents  
- First 100 words: brand + TO/FROM Israel + 24/7 + CTA  
- Trust: equipment, team, bed-to-bed  
- FAQ (3–5) + FAQ schema  
- Outbound: none required; prefer internal  

---

## 5. Content boundaries (SEO = Ads)

Do **not** create SEO hubs for: jobs, EMT courses, MDA, free ambulance, medical tourism packages, domestic discharge ambulance as primary topics.
