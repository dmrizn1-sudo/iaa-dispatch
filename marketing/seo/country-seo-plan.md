# Country SEO Plan — Israel Air Ambulance

**Goal:** Rank for private, high-intent queries connecting each priority country with medical flights **to/from Israel**.

---

## 1. Page model per country

URL: `/routes/{country-slug}`  
Examples: `/routes/usa`, `/routes/united-kingdom`, `/routes/greece`

### Required sections
1. H1: `Air Ambulance {Country} ↔ Israel`  
2. Intro: private families · TO Israel · FROM Israel · 24/7  
3. When to use air ambulance vs medical escort  
4. Major gateway cities (internal links to city pages)  
5. How coordination works (bedside-to-bedside)  
6. FAQ (3–5) + FAQ schema  
7. CTA: Call / WhatsApp  

**Do not** pitch medical tourism packages on these pages.

## 6. Content uniqueness rules

- No duplicate boilerplate across countries — change cities, scenarios, FAQs.  
- Hebrew/IL domestic ambulance content stays off EN international route pages.
