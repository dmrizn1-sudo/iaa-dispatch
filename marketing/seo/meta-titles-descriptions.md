## 5. Image ALT text guidelines

Write ALTs that describe **real content** and include a light keyword only when natural.

| Asset | Example ALT |
|-------|-------------|
| Medical aircraft exterior | Israel Air Ambulance medical aircraft prepared for international patient transfer |
| Cabin / stretcher config | ICU stretcher and medical monitoring equipment inside air ambulance cabin |
| Flight medical crew | Flight physician and critical care paramedic preparing for medical repatriation |
| Ground ambulance handoff | Bedside-to-bedside transfer from ground ambulance to medical aircraft |
| Family coordination (stock carefully) | Coordination team arranging emergency medical flight to Israel |
| Map / routes graphic | Worldwide air ambulance routes to and from Israel |

**Avoid:** keyword stuffing, “image1”, pure brand spam, misleading domestic EMS photos on intl LPs.
