## Article A — Air Ambulance vs Medical Escort: Which Does My Family Need?

**Slug:** `air-ambulance-vs-medical-escort`  
**Meta title:** Air Ambulance vs Medical Escort | Israel Air Ambulance  
**Meta description:** Learn when a private air ambulance is required versus a commercial medical escort for flights to or from Israel. Call 24/7 for guidance.

Families facing an international transfer often hear two options: a **private air ambulance** or a **commercial flight medical escort**. Choosing correctly protects the patient and can avoid unnecessary cost.

### Private air ambulance
A dedicated medical aircraft configured for the patient’s needs — including ICU-level support when required (ventilation, monitoring, infusions). Best when continuous critical care is needed or commercial carriage is not clinically appropriate.

### Commercial medical escort
A physician or critical care paramedic accompanies the patient on a scheduled airline flight when a fit-to-fly assessment supports it. Often suitable for stable patients who still need professional medical presence.

### How Israel Air Ambulance helps
We assess route, clinical status, and urgency for transfers **TO Israel** and **FROM Israel**, then recommend the safest appropriate option — including bedside-to-bedside logistics.
