=== ENGLISH ===
A question we see often in traveler groups:
“A family member is hospitalized in Thailand — how do we get them back to Israel?”

Short answer:
1) Clinical assessment + flight type match
2) Hospital-to-hospital coordination
3) In-flight medical team as needed
4) Family support through arrival in Israel

You can also contact us just to ask what is clinically appropriate — no obligation.
