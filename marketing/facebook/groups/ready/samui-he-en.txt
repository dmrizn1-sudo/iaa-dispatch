Israelis in Koh Samui 🏝️

In a medical emergency on the island, there is an organized path home:
local medical transfer (often via Bangkok) → medical flight / escort to Israel.

Israel Air Ambulance coordinates the full chain 24/7:
bedside in Samui → bedside in Israel.
