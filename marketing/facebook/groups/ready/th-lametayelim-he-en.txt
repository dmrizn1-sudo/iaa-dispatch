If a traveler or family member in Thailand (Bangkok · Koh Samui · Pattaya and more) needs medical repatriation to Israel — Israel Air Ambulance coordinates 24/7:
• ICU air ambulance
• Stretcher flight
• Commercial medical escort (when clinically suitable)

Bedside in Thailand → bedside in Israel.
You can also just ask what fits clinically — no obligation.
