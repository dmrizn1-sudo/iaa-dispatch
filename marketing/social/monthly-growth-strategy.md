# Monthly Growth Strategy — Israel Air Ambulance

**Objective:** Grow qualified private emergency leads while protecting brand trust and lowering wasted ad spend.

---

## 1. Monthly scorecard

| Area | KPIs |
|------|------|
| Google Ads | Spend, qualified leads, **CPQL**, CTR, QS, wasted search terms % |
| Website | LP CVR (click→lead), Call %, WhatsApp %, Form %, LCP mobile |
| SEO | GSC clicks on P0 clusters, new pages indexed, organic leads |
| Social | Reach, profile visits, link clicks, inbound DMs → leads |
| GBP | Calls, direction requests (secondary), website clicks |

---

## 2. Month-by-month focus (rolling)

### Month 1 — Foundation
- Tracking live (calls ≥60s, WhatsApp, forms)  
- Search rebuild live on Priority-1 geos  
- Core LPs live (TO / FROM / ICU / Escort / Contact)  
- Negatives imported; daily search-term mining  
- Social cadence starts; Review Desk approvals  

### Month 2 — Conversion & content
- CRO sprint on mobile sticky CTAs  
- Publish 4+ SEO guides  
- Launch 5+ country route pages  
- Reallocate budget to CPQL winners  
- Refresh RSA assets using asset report  

### Month 3 — Geo & authority
- Tier A city pages live  
- Wave-2 geos in Ads (≤15% budget)  
- Digital PR / partnerships (family stories with consent)  
- Evaluate tCPA readiness (≥30 conv/30d)  
- Quarterly creative refresh (aircraft/crew)

Then repeat cycle: **hygiene → scale winners → expand geo/content**.

---

## 3. Channel growth levers

| Channel | Do more of | Stop / avoid |
|---------|------------|--------------|
| Search Ads | Exact/Phrase winners; city ad groups that convert | Broad Match early; tourism LPs |
| SEO | Route pages + guides with CTA | Domestic EMS / jobs content hubs |
| Instagram | Carousels + route education | Viral trends that break medical tone |
| Facebook | Long-form trust stories | Engagement bait unrelated to flights |
| LinkedIn | Professional education for families/referrers | Hard B2B tender chasing |
| GBP | Weekly posts + photo updates | Misleading local-only EMS category focus |

---

## 4. Creative & proof monthly

- 4 new approved photos (aircraft / crew / equipment / coordination)  
- 1 testimonial (consent)  
- 1 carousel design refresh  
- Update GBP photos  

---

## 5. Budget growth rule

Increase monthly Search budget only when:

1. CPQL stable or improving for 2 consecutive weeks  
2. ≥70% of conversions classified qualified  
3. LP mobile CVR ≥ target floor (8%+)  
4. Coordination team can answer 24/7 without SLA collapse  

If operations are saturated, **do not scale ads** — fix response time first.

---

## 6. Monthly meeting agenda (45 min)

1. CPQL & lead quality review (10)  
2. Search terms / negatives (10)  
3. LP & CRO results (10)  
4. SEO pages shipped (5)  
5. Social approvals & GBP (5)  
6. Next month experiments (5)
