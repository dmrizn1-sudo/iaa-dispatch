# Weekly Posting Strategy — Israel Air Ambulance

**Rule:** Prepare drafts for approval. **Do not auto-publish.**

Platforms: Instagram · Facebook · LinkedIn · Threads · Google Business Profile

---

## 1. Weekly cadence

| Day | Instagram | Facebook | LinkedIn | Threads | GBP |
|-----|-----------|----------|----------|---------|-----|
| Sun | Feed (trust/safety) | Long-form mirror | — | Short take | — |
| Mon | Feed (educational) | Long-form | Professional post | — | GBP update |
| Tue | Story/behind-scenes | — | — | Short take | — |
| Wed | Feed (route/city) | Long-form | Professional post | — | — |
| Thu | Carousel* or feed | Long-form mirror | — | Short take | GBP update |
| Fri | Feed (CTA/emergency) | Long-form | Professional post | — | — |
| Sat | Light educational / route | Mirror | — | Optional | — |

\*Carousel every ~5 days (see library). LinkedIn: **4 posts/week** (Mon/Wed/Fri + one route deep-dive).

---

## 2. Content mix (per week)

| Pillar | Share | Examples |
|--------|-------|----------|
| Educational | 30% | Air ambulance vs escort; fit-to-fly |
| Trust / safety / crew | 25% | ICU equipment; bedside-to-bedside |
| Routes / geo | 25% | City ↔ Israel; country corridors |
| Emergency / CTA | 15% | Holiday emergency; fly patient home |
| Brand / aircraft | 5% | Aircraft photo with simple caption |

Always reinforce: **TO Israel · FROM Israel · private families · 24/7**.

---

## 3. Platform formatting

| Platform | Style |
|----------|-------|
| Instagram | Shorter paragraphs; line breaks; hashtags at end (rotation library) |
| Facebook | Longer narrative; minimal hashtags; strong CTA paragraph |
| LinkedIn | Professional; clinical/logistics clarity; no emoji spam |
| Threads | 1–3 short paragraphs; one CTA |
| GBP | 150–300 words; offer/update; phone CTA; no hashtag stuffing |

---

## 4. Weekly workflow (ops)

1. **Friday:** Pull next week’s posts from `data/posts.json` / Review Desk  
2. **Weekend:** Design creative (aircraft/crew/equipment — approved assets only)  
3. **Monday AM:** Human approval checklist (scope lock, phone, no tourism/jobs)  
4. **Publish manually** to each platform  
5. **Log** hashtag set used (avoid repeat combos 14 days)  
6. **Respond** to comments/DMs within 1 hour when possible (emergency brand)

---

## 5. Approval checklist (every post)

- [ ] Mentions international TO/FROM Israel (or clearly route-based)  
- [ ] Does **not** sell domestic ground ambulance as the hero  
- [ ] No medical tourism package language  
- [ ] No jobs/training/volunteer CTAs  
- [ ] Phone and/or WhatsApp present on CTA posts  
- [ ] Creative is real/approved (no misleading stock)  
- [ ] Hashtags rotated per `hashtag-library.md`  

---

## 6. Engagement & lead handling

- DM intent → move to WhatsApp/phone within minutes  
- Public comments: helpful + invite private contact  
- Never give clinical advice that replaces assessment
